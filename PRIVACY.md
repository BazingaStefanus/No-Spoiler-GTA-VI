# Privacy Policy

No Spoiler for GTA VI (the browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, and the Safari apps for iOS, iPadOS and macOS) does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- The extension reads the text of the web pages you visit only locally on your device, to find the keywords you configured and hide matching content. Page content is never stored or sent anywhere.
- Your settings (keywords, hiding mode, website list, on/off switches) are stored locally in your browser's extension storage on your device.
- The extension and the apps make no network requests, contain no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party code, and require no account.

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/BazingaStefanus/No-Spoiler-GTA-VI/issues.

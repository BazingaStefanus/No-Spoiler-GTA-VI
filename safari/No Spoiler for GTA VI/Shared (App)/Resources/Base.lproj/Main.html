Turn on No Spoiler for GTA VI

The extension runs inside Safari. Turn it on once in Settings and it hides GTA VI spoilers on every website you visit.

  1. Open the Settings app and go to Apps → Safari → Extensions (on iOS 17: Safari → Extensions).
  2. Tap No Spoiler for GTA VI.
  3. Turn on Allow Extension.
  4. Under website permissions, set All Websites (or Other Websites) to Allow. Spoilers can only be hidden on websites the extension may access.
  5. Optional: turn on Allow in Private Browsing.

Using it in Safari

Spoilers are hidden automatically. Tap a hidden area to reveal it. To change keywords, the mode or the per-site switch, open the page menu in Safari’s address bar and tap No Spoiler for GTA VI.
